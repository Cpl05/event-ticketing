Project scope:
Organisers create events (name, venue, date, time, capacity, ticket types and prices).
Attendees browse upcoming events and reserve tickets; sales stop when capacity is reached.
Each ticket gets a unique QR code, shown on screen and downloadable.
A check-in page (usable on a phone) scans or enters the code and marks the ticket as used.
A ticket cannot be checked in twice; invalid or cancelled tickets are rejected with a clear message.
Attendees can cancel a ticket up to 48 hours before the event.
Organiser dashboard: tickets sold, checked in and remaining per event.
